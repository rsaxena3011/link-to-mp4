A little help

Three steps to a saved video

Paste a public video page link, choose your quality and output folder, then select Download MP4. Paste multiple video links on separate lines to build a queue. Keep the helper window open until the work finishes.

Which output should I choose?

Preserve quality copies the available streams into MP4 without re-encoding. If those codecs cannot go into MP4, it stops and tells you. Some MP4s need a modern player such as VLC.

Compatible MP4 uses H.264 video and AAC audio, converting only when the selected streams need it. Conversion takes longer and can change quality. Known HDR sources that need conversion are stopped to avoid silently changing colors. Choose Preserve quality for those.

“Best available” is not the original upload

Most platforms recompress uploads. This app can only save the streams the website makes available. Quality limits are maximum heights, not a promise that a particular resolution exists. Streams with unknown resolution may still be selected. There is no upscaling.

A link did not work?

Try the individual video's page link, check your connection, then update the engine below. Site changes, private videos, login requirements, region restrictions, rate limits, and removed content can prevent downloads. This version does not import login cookies or handle DRM, live streams, or entire playlists.

What does pause do?

Pause queue lets the current video finish and holds the next videos. Cancel stops a specific video and removes its temporary files. Retrying starts from the beginning. Clearing finished removes history entries; it does not delete saved videos.

Where does my data go?

Videos are saved directly to your chosen folder. The queue, links, titles and folder choices stay in this app's local .data folder. Video websites receive the requests needed to fetch your video. Setup and updates contact PyPI and official Deno releases. There is no app analytics or upload service.

Disk space & setup

Downloading and packing MP4 can temporarily need two to three times the final video size. Python 3.10+ is required once. Setup installs the engine, FFmpeg, and a JavaScript runtime when needed. Read README.md for platform-specific setup and uninstall instructions.
