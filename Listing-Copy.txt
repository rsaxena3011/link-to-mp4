FAQ

Is it really free?
Yes. All app features are included. There is no paid tier or subscription.

Does it work as a single HTML file?
The interface is HTML, but cross-site downloading and MP4 processing require the included local Python helper. Start the app with the launcher.

Do my videos pass through your server?
There is no app download/upload server. Your computer requests the video from its source and saves it to your selected folder.

Can it download from absolutely any site?
No. yt-dlp supports many sites, but individual links can be blocked, restricted or unsupported. Sites can also change how they work.

Will I get the original uploaded quality?
Not necessarily. The app downloads the best matching streams the site exposes. Those are often processed versions of the original upload. Preserve quality avoids an extra re-encode when MP4 packing is possible.

Can I choose another drive?
Yes. Choose a writable destination folder or enter its full path. Files save directly there.

Does it overwrite videos?
No. If a filename already exists, it adds a number to the new file.

Does cancel support resuming halfway?
No. Cancel removes job-specific temporary files. Retry starts a fresh download. Pause queue holds the next jobs while the current one finishes.

What if a site stops working?
Try the video's own page link and update the download engine from Help. Login, DRM, region and access restrictions may still prevent downloads.
